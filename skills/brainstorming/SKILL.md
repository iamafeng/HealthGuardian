# Skill: Brainstorming

## Description
A divergent thinking process to explore multiple solutions and refine requirements before any technical decisions are made.

## Instructions
- **Question Everything**: Ask "Why?" and "How?" to surface hidden assumptions.
- **Diverge**: Generate at least 3-5 different approaches to a problem.
- **Categorize**: Group ideas by technical feasibility, user impact, and development time.
- **Converge**: Use a logical framework (e.g., impact vs. effort) to select the best path.
- **Output**: A structured design document that defines the "What" and "Why" before moving to the "How" (Planning).

## Constraints
- Never commit to a solution after the first idea.
- Always document the trade-offs of the chosen approach versus the alternatives.
